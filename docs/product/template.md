## Product position statement

Spynel is a classic, non-AI program that coordinates and oversees external AI/coding harnesses. One human works through one assistant-facing relationship to many agents; the middle agent is the interface relationship, not Spynel itself, and "infinite" is not a literal resource guarantee.
