## Scope

This code of conduct applies to the canonical repository on GitHub: issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and any linked community spaces. It applies to everyone, including maintainers.

## Expectations

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas and evidence, not people.
- **Be constructive.** Point out a concrete problem and, when reasonable, a direction to fix it; demand evidence, not speculative completeness.
- **Be evidence-first.** Substantiate claims about behavior, performance, or security with reproducible commands and results; correct errors promptly instead of defensively.
- **Be patient.** Contributors of all experience levels and language backgrounds are welcome; ask for clarification rather than assuming bad intent.
- **Protect privacy and secrets.** Never share another person's data, tokens, credentials, or workspace identity.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, trolling, personal attacks, and intimidation in any form.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Deliberately destructive or undirected criticism that blocks review without substance.
- Disclosing vulnerabilities or secret material in public channels.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers by emailing the repository owner's registered address (`rrrarrr37r@gmail.com`) or by private message through the repository's issue tracker. Reports are handled confidentially and are acted on by the maintainers.
